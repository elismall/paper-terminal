Works on phone as a home-screen app (manifest.webmanifest + icon-192.png; iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: Chrome ⋮ → Add to Home screen / Install app). **What v0.13.0 added (Eli, 2026-09-29: "a feature on the site that accepts a specific prompt/scenario about any given catalyst and then give a price target prediction or possible entry" · "for option ideas can we include long calls? i only want to include stocks and earnings first ... i will use this new feature for options and swing plays" · "a catalyst may be the rumors of a new product launching etc. dont want to box ourselves in for just earnings"):**
- **Catalyst Scenario panel** (Options tab, panel 40; `lib/catalyst.js`, `routes/catalyst.js`, `js/3j-catalyst.js`), stocks only. You type a ticker + a scenario, optionally the type, direction, event date and holding period (1 / 5 / 10 / 20 trading days). **Jev classifies** the text (new `CATALYST_Q`: kind, direction, size, priced-in; Jev never supplies numbers); if Jev is off or under 40% sure, keywords decide; your picks override both. **The numbers come from this stock's own history around similar past events** (3 years): earnings = SEC 8-K item 2.02 dates with before-open / during / after-close timing from the acceptance time; product / regulatory / deal = SEC full-text search (`efts.sec.gov`, 8-Ks + press-release exhibits, earnings releases excluded) + past Benzinga headlines matching the type (via Alpaca news, up to 40 pages); deal also 8-K items 1.01 / 2.01; rumor / analyst / other = headlines (other = headlines containing your own words); macro = past Fed decision days (built in, checked 2026-09-29) + CPI / jobs days from FRED. Headlines about 5+ stocks are skipped, repeats within 2 sessions merged, and non-earnings events inside an earnings week left out. Per event: gap, reaction-day move, move over the holding period, vs SPY, 5-day run-up. Fewer than 5 events → the graded range falls back to normal volatility (±1.28 sd, an 80% band) and it says so. Output: stats, **swing plan** (entry zone price to −0.5 ATR, stop 1.5 ATR, target = median move of past events in that direction, else 0.8 sd; reward/risk and a warning under 1×), **long call / long put** at three strikes past the event date with "past events cleared" per breakeven, the options' **implied move** (at-the-money straddle), warnings (options pricing more than the usual move = expensive + IV drop after the event; estimated earnings inside the window; Jev says priced in). **Every scenario is saved** (`catalyst/log-v1.json`, last 150) and **graded** once its holding period is over (move vs the range, right direction, swing target or stop first); scorecard by catalyst type in the panel. Never places orders (trade buttons only open the ticket).
- **Option ideas upgraded** (`lib/options.js` shared chain code, `routes/options.js`): new **long call** and **long put** ideas (`strategy=call|put`, optional `dte` 10–120): in / at / out of the money (~0.70 / 0.50 / 0.30 delta) with cost, breakeven, move needed, delta, decay per day ($), IV and value at the target price at expiry. Every idea (spreads and cash-secured puts too) flags the next earnings report when the expiry spans it (estimated from SEC filings), and long options also show the implied move by then. Detail drawer: new "Long call / Long put" button next to "Build option idea". The Options tab form now lives in a fixed shell, so data refreshes never wipe what you typed.

## 5. The bot

**Schedule (UTC; Hobby precision ±59 min):** `/api/bot` 14:40 (≈10:40 ET, stocks + crypto), `/api/bot-mid` 16:40 (≈12:40 ET), `/api/bot-pm` 18:30 (≈2:30 ET), Mon–Fri; `/api/bot-crypto` 23:00 daily (≈7 pm ET, crypto only). **Hourly crypto checks (v0.9.0):** `/api/bot-check` at :05 every UTC hour not covered by a main run (0–13, 15, 17, 19–22 daily; 14, 16, 18 on weekends) = `runBot(req,'chk')`: crypto only (swing-coin stops/targets, DCA crypto deals: dip buys, take profits, trailing floors, hard exits, new crypto deals), no stocks, no warm-up training (uses the cached training), no hold review, doesn't overwrite the last-run record. Manual (Bot tab, passcode): `?run=1&only=stocks&max=1|2|3`, `?run=1&check=1` ("Run crypto check now"), `?run=1&only=dca`, `?dry=1` (preview). **Gap check:** `/api/gapcheck` crons 12:00 and 13:00 UTC Mon–Fri; acts once between 8:00 and 9:25 AM ET (DST-proof); stock names gapping ≥ 1 ATR either way are skipped for new entries that day (up to 8 shown in the run's `skipped`); holdings gapping ≥ 0.75 ATR or through their stop trigger a push alert. Extended-hours limits (Alpaca): limit orders only, no brackets, stops don't trigger pre-market, so the gap check only warns/skips, it never trades.

**Order ids (the bot's memory; no database):** stocks `tbbot-YYYYMMDD-SYM-setup-scNN-hN-s<origStop>[-jNN[eXrYdZ]][-f]` (the original stop is kept so R stays honest after a breakeven move; `origStop()` in botcore parses it), time exit `tbexit-…`, breakeven exit `tbexit-…-be`, stop PATCH `tbbe-…`, re-armed OCO `tbprot-…`; crypto entry `tbcry-YYYYMMDD-SOLUSD-setup-scNN-hN-s<stop>-t<target>[-jNN][-p]`, standing stop `tbcstop-…` (`…-be…` once moved to breakeven), crypto exits `tbcx-…-tgt|time|stop|be-…`.

**Breakeven rule (`BE` in lib/perf.js: `{ on:true, at:0.5, cushionR:0.05 }`):** once the high since entry (15-minute bars) reaches entry + 50% of the way to target, the stop moves to entry + max(0.05R, 2× round-trip cost). Stocks: PATCH the stop leg; crypto: cancel + re-place the stop-limit. If price has already fallen back to that level at run time, sell at market. Training (`simulate`) applies the same rule from the next bar, exit reason "breakeven". Eli's goal: consistent money, never let a good trade turn into a loss.

**DCA bot (lib/dca.js, v0.6.0; coins/slots v0.10.0; lab + bull-run mode v0.11.0; 3Commas-style deals, paper):** own budget `DCA.budget` $20,000, split 70% crypto ($14k: up to 10 deals of $1,400, 2 of the slots reserved for meme coins DOGE SHIB PEPE BONK WIF TRUMP; coins = the DCA lab's picks when the lab found picking helps, else the hand list BTC ETH SOL XRP LINK AVAX LTC BCH ADA DOT AAVE UNI + the memes) / 30% ETFs ($6k: SPY QQQ IWM DIA XLK, up to 3 deals of $2,000) — Eli chose 70/30 crypto/stocks. A deal = base market buy (15% of the deal since v0.13.1) → up to n dip buys (3 since v0.13.1; they share the other 85%) resting as limit orders below the start (gaps d%, gap scale k, size scale v; the next 3 (crypto) / 2 (ETF) rest at a time) → one limit take-profit for the whole position at avg × (1 + tp%), never under the price that nets 0.5% after costs (v0.13.1) → deal closes, leftovers canceled next run. Hard exit: market sell if price ≤ last dip level × (1 − x%). Start rule f: a = right away, t = daily close above 200-day SMA, d = trend + hourly RSI(14) < 40. Settings trained daily on 365 days of hourly bars (432 crypto combos incl. trailing, 144 ETF, all with 3 dip buys since v0.13.1) by simulating the real run schedule (crypto: every hour since v0.9.0; ETFs: 14/16/18 UTC weekdays, day orders); pick = highest return ÷ max drawdown with ≥20 deals and positive return; walk-forward (pick on first 2/3, test on last 1/3) and a typical no-stop 3Commas setup are reported for honesty. Not proven → half the deals. Cached ~20 h in Blob. Order ids: `tbdca-<yymmddHHMM>-<SYM>-<b|sN|tp|ts|hx>-u<base $>-b<start px>-n-d-k-v-t-x-f[-r<trail %>][-p<first buy % of the deal, v0.13.1>][-jNN…][-h<funding>][-q<money>] (tags on the base order only)-z<unique>`; bull-run core `tbhodl-<yymmddHHMM>-<SYM>-<b|x>-z<unique>` (all deal params live in every id; deal state is rebuilt from positions + ids each run). Ownership rule shared by all bots: a symbol belongs to whichever bot's order bought it most recently (`lastBuyOf`); swing crypto and DCA never manage each other's coins; DCA runs after the swing bots in each run so both can't buy the same coin at once; the unused DCA budget is counted as used for the swing bots' no-margin cap. Manual: DCA tab or Bot tab "Run DCA now" (`?run=1&only=dca`). Dust rule (v0.7.0): a position worth under $1 (e.g. 1e-9 XRP left after a take profit) counts as no position, so the deal closes and its leftover dip buys are canceled. The DCA tab (D2) shows an explicit rules card (per market: coins, deals, start rule, first buy $, every dip level % and $, resting count, take profit, hard exit from the start, backtest) computed live from the trained settings. Settings before v0.13.1 (retrain 2026-09-28 1:51 PM ET): crypto 6 dips from −2.5% ×1.4, size ×1.8, trailing TP from +1% (1% trail), hard exit 5%, uptrend start; ETFs 6 dips from −1% even gaps, size ×1.8, TP +0.5%, hard exit 3%, start ASAP. v0.13.1 retrains on 3-dip-buy settings (`dca/train-v6.json`); settings retrain daily and can change. Benchmark tab: "Swing bots vs DCA bot · live paper" (same dates, realized + open); DCA training is on the DCA tab (D8) since v0.12.0.

**Trailing take profit (v0.9.0, crypto DCA only):** training grid adds `tr` ∈ {0, 1, 2}% for crypto (864 combos; ETFs stay `tr:0`, 288). With `tr>0`: activation `act = avg × (1+tp%)`; floor `minF = avg × (1+tp/200)`; once the best price since the last fill (`peak`, from 1-hour bars) ≥ act, the sell floor = max(peak × (1−tr%), minF). Each run/check: price above floor → keep/raise a GTC stop_limit (`ts`) at the floor (limit floor × 0.99); price under floor but ≥ minF → market sell; under minF → limit sell at minF. The simulator models exactly this (stop fills at floor × 0.998 between runs). DCA tab: deal cards show "Trailing take profit: starts at X" / "active: sells under Y", chart lines TRAIL STARTS / TRAIL FLOOR; the rules card and the Open-now list say it too. Crypto schedule in training = hourly (matches the checks); ETF runs stay 14/16/18 UTC weekdays.

**Safety (v0.7.0, lib/control.js, `RISK = { maxDrawdown: 0.08, dailyStop: -0.02, staleStockMin: 20, staleCryptoMin: 45, lockSec: 320 }`):** every non-preview run takes a Blob lock (a second overlapping run stands down; 320 s outlasts the 300 s function limit since v0.12.1; if the store can't confirm the lock the run goes to level 3). Then the ladder: 1 Normal; 2 Caution (Jev in gate mode unreachable → rules only); 3 Hold = no new trades this run (down ≥ 2% today; SPY last trade > 20 min old in market hours → no stocks/DCA; BTC > 45 min → no crypto/DCA); 4 Stopped = no new trades until Resume (Pause pressed, `BOT_PAUSED=true`, or equity ≥ 8% under the 1-month high → auto-pause + push). Management (exits, stops, breakeven, DCA dip buys/take profits/hard exits, leftover cancels) runs at every level. Preview runs follow the ladder too but never persist an auto-pause. Emergency stop (Bot tab, tap twice): pause + cancel every bot order (tb* ids, or any order on a bot-owned symbol) + close every bot-owned position at market; manual positions/orders untouched. **Jev (live in shadow since v0.8.0):** each NEW entry candidate (stocks, swing crypto, DCA base) gets the rules' numbers plus the chart snapshot and 7 questions: quality 0–3 + confidence, trend 0–3, regime, stretched?, selling pressure?, volume ok?, sharp-drop risk; stored in the entry order id as `-j<q×10>e<ext>r<rev>d<down>` (tenths 0–9, e.g. `-j24e3r1d2`; old `-jNN` still parses); flag = stretched or selling pressure ≥ 0.7 → half size in gate mode; the scorecard also splits flagged vs not; gate: drop risk ≥ 0.75 or (score < 1 and confidence ≥ 0.6) → skip; score < 1.6 or confidence < 0.4 → half size (DCA: skip only, never resize). The Jev tab scorecard (on Benchmark before v0.12.0) buckets closed trades/deals by entry score (and flagged/not). After each real run the hold review asks Jev about every open swing position (hold 0–3, keeps going, fading, drop risk) and shows it on the trade cards as "AI view (shadow)"; it never moves stops or sells. Jev reads charts as numbers (chartState), not images.

**Bot tab tiles (v0.9.0):** Stock trades today, Crypto buys today, Open now (swing + DCA count and open P&L), Closed today (count + realized) are buttons; each opens a list under the tiles (Open now rows jump to their card/chart). Lists come from `/api/botview` `today.lists {closed, stocks, crypto}` (New York day; events carry `bot` and `entry`). Rows are a 190px | 1fr | 92px grid on desktop and stack on phones; tested for no overlap/overflow at 1400/390/320 px.

**Notifications:** after every non-dry run `botNotify` sends: buys, sells, stops moved to breakeven, DCA deals started / hard exits, and fills since the last check (stop/target, DCA dip buys and take profits) (fills between runs arrive at the next run). More than 4 events → one summary notification. Settings → Phone notifications: Turn on / Send test / Turn off.

**Stocks (runStocks):** trains on ~200 liquid US stocks (16 stop/target/hold settings, pick steadiest mean R ÷ std R with 60+ trades and positive expectancy; score floor from buckets; blocks losing setups). Manages: time exits after the trained hold; re-arms any bot position whose stop/target are missing or day-only as a GTC OCO. Enters: long setups, bracket market orders **GTC**, 0.5% equity risk (filler 0.25% to reach 3/day), max 6 new/day, 15 positions, 10% per name, total positions ≤ equity (no margin), skip if already up > 0.75 ATR, stand down after −2% day.

**Crypto (runCrypto):** swing ENTRIES only when `CRYPTO_SWING=on` (off since v0.9.0; with it off the crypto backtest is skipped and the run returns a note; existing swing coins are still managed). 13 coins (SHIB excluded), trained separately (0.25%/side costs, 40+ trades needed, `proven` flag; no entries if unproven). 0.25% equity risk, max 2 new/day, 4 coins, 15% of equity, ≤ equity overall. After each buy fills: GTC stop-limit sell (limit 1% under stop). Each run: sells at market if price ≥ target or hold (calendar days) passed; re-places a missing stop.
